Models, training and earlier work

Work alongside the four deliverables: an allocation model for sterile logistics, DRG estimates for budgeting and planning, and Power BI support and training.

Allocation model for sterile logistics

#

The assignment was to distribute the expected benefits of sterile logistics between clinics. I rejected direct allocation by service use because the underlying data could not support it, and turned the task into an allocation model with weighted, indirect indicators and visible assumptions.

I designed five workbooks: four branch models and one consolidation. Formulas and weights are visible and can be adjusted, and Power Query moves data from the branches to the consolidation with a controlled schema. The core deliberately has no VBA, so that controllers can examine and adjust the assumptions without depending on a developer.

The model was completed in early 2026 and handed over to controllers. Organisational use and the final weights have not been settled.

DRG estimates for the clinic's planning

#

In the staff function of the Division of Paediatric and Adolescent Medicine I produced estimates of DRG by section in 2023, meaning activity measured in diagnosis-related groups. The estimates were delivered and used in budgeting and planning. I used Stata, R and Power Query for data preparation and time series analysis.

The work took place before the forecasting project and is a separate deliverable.

Power BI infrastructure and training

#

In autumn 2025 I helped the department responsible for BI at the hospital to put much of its Power BI infrastructure in place. Over several working sessions I trained technical staff in Power BI, semantic models and model optimisation, and I contributed substantially to their first modernised report.

Internal BI workshop

#

I led an internal workshop for colleagues with a background in finance and health economics. We went through process mapping, data transformation, star schema, semantic models, Power Query, optimisation of DAX, data quality and a plan for further work.
