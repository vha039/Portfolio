Vegard Håland

Adviser, Finance and ICT · Oslo University Hospital

I modernised the hospital's budget tool, integrating staffing data previously copied manually. It was used across the hospital for the 2026 and 2027 budgets. I also designed the management BI model, led the forecasting architecture redesign and co-developed the hospital's standard benefits methodology.

Budget tool

My responsibility: Integrated staffing data and was the only person who coded the changes in both rounds.

Status: Used across the whole hospital in the budget processes for 2026 and 2027.

Management BI

My responsibility: Designed almost all of the model behind the reports. Colleagues led content and visual design.

Status: In operation until around February 2026, when new reports covered the need.

Forecasting architecture

My responsibility: Phase I: architecture, integration and several models, in a team. Phase II: led the redesign on my own, with coding agents.

Status: The redesign is complete. The forecasting solution is in development, not in production.

Benefits methodology

My responsibility: Co-developer in a team of four: research, economics and method. Wrote all the VBA in the benefits database.

Status: Adopted as the hospital standard. Still in use about 2.5 years later.

Other work

Allocation model for sterile logistics, completed and handed over to controllers in early 2026. DRG estimates for budgeting and planning in 2023. Internal BI workshop. Power BI infrastructure and training for another department.
