Background

Economics and practical systems work

My role is Adviser, Finance and ICT at Oslo University Hospital, working with finance, staffing, activity, patient flow and capacity.

I joined the hospital in March 2023 and worked in the staff function of the Division of Paediatric and Adolescent Medicine (Barne- og ungdomsklinikken) until the end of November that year. Within about three months I had taken over the whole monthly close workflow assigned to me. Towards the end of the period I carried out the monthly close independently, including accruals and reconciliation, quality-assured it and sent monthly reports to managers in the clinic.

On my own initiative I made tools in Excel and VBA to convert and validate old posting lines into the new accounting format, and I learned VBA from scratch through the work. Finance advisers in several clinics used the tools shortly after the changeover.

Since December 2023 I have worked in the Analytics Department (Analyseavdelingen), where I was part of the group of four that established the department. There I have worked on the budget tool, management BI, forecasting and benefits methodology, sometimes with main responsibility for the code, at other times as part of a team.

My education in economics gave me a foundation in econometrics, time series analysis and the assessment of resource use and incentives.
